✅ DYNO Key Features (the “Why Choose” / Benefit Points)

On the DYNO brand page, several selling-points / feature blocks highlight why DYNO is premium. 
STEK Automotive

Hydrophobic: The film has advanced water-repellent properties — water beads up and rolls off, taking dirt/contaminants along, making cleaning easier and maintaining shine. 
STEK Automotive

Self-Healing: Minor scratches and blemishes disappear over time (via heat-activated or film properties), keeping the surface smooth and new. 
STEK Automotive

Reliable Protection: Options of 8 mil or 10 mil thickness to give “ultimate protection from scratches and environmental damage.” 
STEK Automotive

Stain Resistant: Film resists stains, helping maintain the car’s “immaculate” look. 
STEK Automotive

Ultra-Gloss Finish / Clear Visibility: For the color PPF — delivers vibrant hues and brilliant shine; enhances aesthetic while protecting paint. 
STEK Automotive

These features communicate both functional protection (scratch / chip / environment / stain / water) and aesthetic enhancement (gloss, clarity, clean look), aligning with a “premium + luxury + protection + style” brand promise. 
STEK Automotive
